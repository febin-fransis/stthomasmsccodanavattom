Coordinator's Message

Message from the Central Coordinator

Renewing the domestic Church through neighborhood fellowship and apostolic solidarity

V. C. Mathai

Central Coordinator • Kudumba Koottayma
“

"The Kudumba Koottayma reflects the Apostolic Church described in the Book of Acts, where believers gathered in private homes to break bread, study the Apostles' doctrine, and pray with joyful sincerity. Our five ward units ensure that no family in St. Thomas Parish ever feels forgotten or alone. In moments of illness, bereavement, or financial distress, the ward family is the first to arrive with prayer and practical assistance."

In United Parish Fellowship,
V. C. Mathai • Central Coordinator, Kudumba Koottayma
