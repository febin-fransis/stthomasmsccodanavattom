Director's Exhortation

Message from the Spiritual Director

Standing in awe before the Holy of Holies as consecrated heralds of the altar

Rev. Fr. John Kizhakekkara

Spiritual Director & Vicar
“

"To stand inside the Madbaha — the Holy Sanctuary behind the veil — is an extraordinary grace reserved for those called to assist the Holy Qurbono. Like the angels who minister around the throne of the Lamb, our altar servers carry the censer (Dhoopakutti), raise the ceremonial liturgical fans (Marvahas), bear the processional cross, and handle the sacred vessels. I charge our servers to maintain spotless personal holiness, punctual reverence, and a prayerful heart open to God's priestly call."

With Pastoral Blessings,
Rev. Fr. John Kizhakekkara • Vicar & Spiritual Director

Feast Day Raaza

Carrying the ceremonial Marvahas (seraphic fans), incense, and crucifix in solemn raaza procession.

Liturgical Rubrics Training

Become an Altar Server

Boys who have received their First Holy Communion and demonstrate reverence and dedication are invited to enroll. Regular liturgical training and guild meetings take place on the second Saturday of every month at 3:30 PM in the Church.
