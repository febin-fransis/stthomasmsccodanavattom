Headmaster's Message

Message from the Headmaster

Guiding young minds toward spiritual maturity, scriptural wisdom, and liturgical life

George Varghese

Headmaster • Catechetical Ministry
“

"Catechesis is not merely teaching academic facts about religion; it is initiating young hearts into a personal, loving friendship with Jesus Christ. At St. Thomas Sunday School, our dedicated team of 12 catechists labors with love to instruct our children in Sacred Scripture, Catholic morals, and the profound depth of our West Syriac liturgy. We urge all parents to partner with us in prayer, family devotions, and punctuality."

In Loving Dedication to Christ's Little Ones,
George Varghese • Headmaster, St. Thomas Sunday School

Sunday School Classes

Bible Kalotsavam

Parish and diocesan level competitions in Bible recitation, Christian elocution, and singing.

Vacation Bible School (VBS)

Annual 5-day joy-filled summer faith camp featuring music, crafts, action songs, and spiritual workshops.

Enroll Your Child in Sunday School

Admissions for the new academic year open each May following the Easter season. Classes run every Sunday from 10:15 AM to 12:00 PM for all children aged 5 to 17 years.
