President's Message

Message from the Prayer Groups President

Renewing the domestic Church through intimate neighborhood prayer and spiritual solidarity

Sri. V. C. Mathai

President & Central Coordinator
“

"The Christian home is the first school of faith. In our three patronal prayer groups — St. Thomas, St. Mary's, and St. Joseph — families assemble in rotation to chant the evening office, meditate on the Word of God, and lift one another in prayer. When our families pray together, bonds are healed, sorrows are comforted, and the grace of Christ overflows into the whole parish. We invite every household to actively participate."

In Prayerful Fellowship & Love,
Sri. V. C. Mathai • President, Kudumba Koottayma Central Council
