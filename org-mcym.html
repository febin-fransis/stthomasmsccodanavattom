President's Address

Message from the MCYM President

Youth leadership, spiritual vitality, and apostolic brotherhood in Christ

Jerin Mathew

President • MCYM Odanavattom
“

"The Malankara Catholic Youth Movement is the vibrant heartbeat of our parish. Guided by our motto 'To the Heart of Christ through St. Thomas', we strive to be joyful witnesses in our colleges, workplaces, and families. Firmly rooted in our ancient Syriac liturgical heritage and ignited with zeal for charity, we welcome every young person of our parish to grow with us in faith, leadership, and fellowship."

In Youthful Faith & Solidarity,
Jerin Mathew • Unit President, MCYM Odanavattom
