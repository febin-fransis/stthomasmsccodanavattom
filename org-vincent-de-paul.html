Conference President's Message

Message from the Conference President

The Christian imperative of seeing Christ in our impoverished and vulnerable neighbors

Philipose Mathai

Conference President • SSVP Odanavattom
“

"As disciples of Blessed Frédéric Ozanam and St. Vincent de Paul, our calling is simple yet radical: to love not merely in words, but through concrete personal contact with those who suffer. We do not judge, nor do we disclose who receives aid; we visit families in absolute confidentiality, offering life-saving medical aid, rice, house repairs, and educational help. We thank our parishioners whose quiet weekly contributions make Christ's love tangible."

In Vincentian Humility & Service,
Philipose Mathai • President, St. Thomas Conference SSVP
