Animator's Message

Message from the Unit Animator

Living the virtues of silent diligence, righteous stewardship, and sacrificial love for the family

Sri. Babu K. John

Animator & Unit President
“

"Peace of Christ to you all! Saint Joseph, the just man and guardian of the Redeemer, was a pillar of quiet faith and resolute action. He protected the Child Jesus and our Blessed Mother with utmost fidelity. In St. Joseph Prayer Group, our families come together on the third Sunday of each month to draw strength from his holy example. We encourage fathers to be spiritual leaders in their homes, guide our youth to value honest labor and integrity, and ensure that no family in our ward feels forgotten or isolated in times of hardship. We pray that St. Joseph’s powerful heavenly intercession watches over every hearth and threshold in Odanavattom."

In St. Joseph's Intercessory Care,
Sri. Babu K. John • Animator, St. Joseph Prayer Group (Unit 3)

Geographical Coverage

Covering the western parish sector, south Odanavattom road, Valakom junction approaches, and neighboring plantations.

Gathering Routine

Every 3rd Sunday at 5:00 PM. Highlights the Litany of St. Joseph, Scripture reflections, family blessings, and social sharing.

Service & Brotherhood

Conducts voluntary home repairs for aged parishioners, worker career mentorship, and coordinating relief aid during distress.

Devoted Fellowship

Activities & Photo Gallery

Moments from monthly family prayer hours, St. Joseph Novenas, altar animations, and charity outreaches

Home Gathering

Monthly Home Fellowship

Ward families gathered at a member home for Scripture study, intercession, and fellowship tea.

Patronal Novena

Feast of St. Joseph Novena

Ward members assembling for nine-day special litanies prior to the feast of St. Joseph, protector of the Church.

Ward Liturgy

Animated Sunday Holy Qurbono

Unit 3 families leading the congregational singing, reading the epistles, and offering prayers of the faithful.

Outreach Service

Home Assistance & Visitation

Unit members visiting homes of elderly couples to pray, assist in yard cleaning, and provide festive food baskets.

Belong to the Western Ward?

If you live in western or southern Odanavattom, come be part of our home gatherings! Reach out to Animator Sri. Babu K. John or contact the parish vicar to find the date and location of the next meeting.
