Our Parish Heritage (Since 1949)

A Legacy of Reunion and Faith

St. Thomas Malankara Catholic Church at Odanavattom was established in 1949 under the visionary pastoral leadership of the Servant of God, Archbishop Geevarghese Mar Ivanios. Following the historic Reunion Movement of September 20, 1930, the faithful families of Odanavattom united under the universal communion of the Catholic Church while preserving their authentic Antiochene West Syriac rite.

Over the decades, guided by great church fathers including Archbishop Benedict Mar Gregorios and Moran Mor Cyril Baselios, our church grew from a humble thatched chapel to a magnificent spiritual center. In 2024, our parish solemnly commemorated its Platinum Jubilee (75 Years) of devotion, education, and social charity under the pastoral care of the Major Archdiocese of Trivandrum.

Milestones of Grace
  • 1949: Foundation stone and first Holy Qurbono celebration.
  • 1974: Silver Jubilee and expansion of the Parish Catechism center.
  • 1999: Golden Jubilee and consecration of the renovated Holy Madbaho.
  • 2024: Platinum Jubilee celebrations with Major Archbishop-Catholicos Moran Mor Baselios Cardinal Cleemis.

St. Thomas the Apostle (Mar Thoma)

The Apostle of India

Saint Thomas, one of the Twelve Apostles of Jesus Christ, arrived at the ancient seaport of Muziris (Kodungallur), Kerala, in AD 52. He proclaimed the Gospel of salvation, baptized many, and established seven Christian communities (Ezharappallikal) along the Malabar coast.

The Syro-Malankara Catholic Church traces its apostolic lineage to the faith, liturgy, and martyrdom of Mar Thoma at St. Thomas Mount, Chennai in AD 72. His enduring confession of faith—"My Lord and my God!" (Moran v'Alahan)—remains the bedrock of our parish spirituality.

Parish Feast Day: Celebrated annually on July 3rd (Dukrono of Mar Thoma) with solemn Holy Qurbono, liturgical Rasa procession, and community love-feast (Nercha).

Parish Ministries & Organizations

  • Sunday School & Catechism (Darsanaveedhi)

    Spiritual, moral, and liturgical formation for children from Kindergarten through 12th Standard every Sunday after the 9:00 AM Holy Mass.

  • MCYM (Malankara Catholic Youth Movement)

    Vibrant youth wing dedicated to faith witness, charitable works, community outreach, and parish choir ministry.

  • MCA (Malankara Catholic Association)

    Parish men's association supporting parish administration, community upliftment, and church festivities.

  • Mathrusangham & Legion of Mary

    Devout women of the parish visiting elderly, reciting the family Rosary, and supporting sick parishioners.

  • Society of St. Vincent de Paul (SSVP)

    Premier charitable fellowship providing food rations, emergency medical support, dialysis assistance, and education aid for needy families.
