Animator's Message

Message from the Unit Animator

Following Mother Mary's path of humble surrender, faithful prayer, and steadfast domestic charity

Smt. Sosamma Mathew

Animator & Unit President
“

"Warm greetings in the precious name of our Lord Jesus and our Blessed Mother Mary! As Mother Mary pondered God's holy mysteries in her heart and gathered the Apostles in prayer at the Upper Room, our St. Mary's Prayer Group unites the households of eastern Odanavattom in constant spiritual closeness. On every second Sunday, we sit together in each other's homes, pray the Holy Rosary with meditation on the Gospel, and chant the sweet Syriac Theotokos hymns. When a family faces illness, grief, or financial hardship, our prayer unit becomes an immediate source of consolation and practical care. May Mary's maternal mantle continue to shield and bless every family in our parish."

In Mother Mary's Maternal Love,
Smt. Sosamma Mathew • Animator, St. Mary's Prayer Group (Unit 2)

Geographical Coverage

Covering the scenic eastern hills, valley settlements along Nedumoncavu route, and connected agrarian households.

Gathering Routine

Every 2nd Sunday at 5:00 PM. Highlights the Holy Rosary, Scripture readings, Marian chant meditations, and community fellowship.

Devotions & Care

Active during the 8-Day Lent (Ettunombu), 10-day Rosary month devotions, visiting elderly mothers, and extending compassionate emergency aid.

Marian Fellowship in Action

Activities & Photo Gallery

Moments from family Rosaries, 8-Day Lent fasting gatherings, harvest offerings, and parish festivals

Home Rosary

Monthly Family Rosary

Ward families gathered before the home altar chanting the Rosary and lifting up children's examinations and health needs.

Ettunombu Feast

8-Day Lent (Ettunombu)

Intensive prayer meetings and fasting fellowship held in preparation for the feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Ward Liturgy

Animated Sunday Qurbono

St. Mary's Unit actively participating as altar acolytes, choir assistants, and leading prayer intentions during parish Qurbono.

Agape & Charity

Harvest Agape & Charity

Bringing agricultural produce and festive treats to share with widows, elderly parish members, and neighborhood families.

Belong to the Eastern Ward?

If your residence is located in eastern Odanavattom or the valley sector, we invite you to attend or host our monthly prayer meeting. Reach out to Animator Smt. Sosamma Mathew or contact the church office to participate.
