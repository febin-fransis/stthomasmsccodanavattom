Presidential Message

Message from the MCMF President

Maternal intercession, Christian family holiness, and living Marian charity

Marykutty Abraham

President • MCMF Odanavattom Unit
“

"The Catholic mother is the spiritual heart of the domestic sanctuary. Following the radiant exemplar of our Blessed Mother Mary, the Malankara Catholic Mother's Forum unites the women of our parish in steadfast prayer, sisterly solidarity, and selfless service. We join hands in intercession for our children, our families, and our clergy. Through daily family Rosaries and deeds of quiet charity, we strive to make Christ's peace dwell in every home."

With Motherly Love & Prayers,
Marykutty Abraham • Unit President, MCMF
