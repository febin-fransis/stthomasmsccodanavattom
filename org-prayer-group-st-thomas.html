Animator's Message

Message from the Unit Animator

Fostering apostolic faith, heartfelt prayer, and brotherly communion across our neighborhood homes

Sri. Georgekutty Joseph

Animator & Unit President
“

"Praise be to Jesus Christ! As the St. Thomas Prayer Group, our households are blessed to bear the name of our glorious Father in Faith, Apostle St. Thomas. When we open our family doors on the first Sunday of each month, we do not merely gather for social fellowship; we welcome the Living Christ into our midst. Our shared recitation of the Malankara Sandhya Namaskaram, heartfelt intercession for parish members undergoing trials, and reading of the Holy Gospel infuse spiritual vitality into our children and elders alike. May the fearless confession of Mar Thoma Sleeha — 'My Lord and My God' — guide each of our families in faith and holiness."

With Prayerful Regards & Sincere Love,
Sri. Georgekutty Joseph • Animator, St. Thomas Prayer Group (Unit 1)

Geographical Scope

Encompasses Central Parish Rectory Ward, Church Junction, North Odanavattom road, and connected settlement houses.

Gathering Routine

Every 1st Sunday at 5:00 PM. Includes West Syriac Sandhya Namaskaram, Gospel meditation, sharing of ward needs, and refreshments.

Pastoral Care & Charity

Conducts regular home visitations to the bedridden, mutual financial aid during medical emergencies, and parish feast rally representation.

Moments in Fellowship

Activities & Photo Gallery

Faithful gatherings, Bible reflections, feast rallies, and mutual fraternal love in action

Home Gathering

Monthly Family Gathering

Parishioners assembled at a member's residence for the chanting of Syriac evening prayers and scripture sharing.

Dukhrana Feast

Patronal Feast Novena

Special 10-day intercessory prayer sessions prior to the July 3 Dukhrana feast of our patron Apostle St. Thomas.

Ward Liturgy

Animated Sunday Holy Qurbono

St. Thomas prayer group families leading the readings, liturgical hymns, and offertory procession at the parish altar.

Feast Rally

Parish Feast Presentation

Unit 1 members presenting traditional devotional music and banner rallies during the annual parish festival.

Belong to the St. Thomas Ward?

If your family resides in the central church vicinity or north Odanavattom, you are warmly invited to host or attend our next monthly home prayer gathering. Contact Animator Sri. Georgekutty Joseph or the parish office for the upcoming venue.
