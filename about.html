Our Parish Story

Parish Milestones & Spiritual History

Trace our journey from humble beginnings to a vibrant ecclesial center

1949 PARISH INCEPTION

First Community Formed

The initial group of Syro-Malankara Catholic faithful in Odanavattom gathered together to initiate sacramental and community life, laying down the deep foundations of our parish.

1960 MAY 19

Parish Established & Blessed

Officially established as a parish church. Blessed by the saintly Archbishop of Trivandrum, the Late Moran Mor Benedict Mar Gregorios, cementing its place in the Major Archeparchy.

2010 MARCH 4

Church Rebuilt & Blessed

The church building was gracefully rebuilt under the visionary leadership of Rev. Fr. Alex Kalappila. Blessed by Moran Mor Baselios Cardinal Cleemis, the Major Archbishop-Catholicos of the Syro-Malankara Catholic Church.

OUR PROTECTOR & INTERCESSOR

St. Thomas the Apostle (Maran Slikha)

Tradition holds that St. Thomas, one of the twelve apostles of Jesus Christ, arrived in Kerala in 52 AD. He preached the Gospel and established seven and a half churches, planting the seeds of the indigenous Mar Thoma Christian faith in India.

Our parish bears his saintly name with absolute devotion. The annual feast of St. Thomas (Dukhrana) on July 3rd is celebrated with deep Fast preparations, Holy Liturgy, and sacred community processions.
