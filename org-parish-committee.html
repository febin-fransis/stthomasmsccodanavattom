Message from the President & Vicar

Guiding parish life in synergy with the elected lay leadership and pastoral council

Rev. Fr. John Kizhakekkara

President & Parish Vicar
“

"The Parish Committee embodies the co-responsibility of clergy and faithful taught by the Holy Catholic Church. Working in harmonious collaboration with the Vicar, the Trustee, the Secretary, and council representatives, our mission is to steward the material assets, sacred traditions, and welfare of every soul entrusted to St. Thomas Church. Through financial transparency, prayerful consensus, and visionary development, we build up the Body of Christ in Odanavattom."

In Christian Leadership & Pastoral Communion,
Rev. Fr. John Kizhakekkara • Vicar & Council President
