Message from the MCTF President

The noble calling of teaching as an apostolate of truth, ethics, and human development

Prof. Sunny Jacob

President • MCTF Odanavattom Unit
“

"Teaching is not merely a profession; it is a sacred apostolic vocation. As Catholic educators in schools, colleges, and Sunday schools, we are called to shape not only intellectual capability, but the moral conscience and spiritual depth of the young generation. Through MCTF, we come together for mutual encouragement, pedagogical reflection, carrier guidance clinics, and ensuring every student in our parish reaches their God-given potential."

In Educational Fellowship & Faith,
Prof. Sunny Jacob • Unit President, MCTF
