Message from the Choir Master

Entering sacred mystery through the timeless melodies of the West Syriac tradition

Mathew K. Varghese

Choir Master • Liturgical Cantor
“

"As St. Augustine declared, 'He who sings prays twice.' In the Syro-Malankara Catholic liturgy, music is not performance or entertainment — it is theology sung aloud. Through the venerable modes of Beth Gazo and ancient Syriac hymns, our choir leads the assembled body of Christ before the throne of God. We invite voices of all generations to unite in discipline, reverence, and harmony."

In Choral Harmony & Prayer,
Mathew K. Varghese • Choir Master, St. Thomas Church
