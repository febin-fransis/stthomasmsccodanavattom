VICAR'S WELCOME MESSAGE

Rev. Fr. John Kizhakekkara

"Blessings and peace to you in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. St. Thomas Parish, Odanavattom, stands as a community of love, traditional Syrian spirituality, and active Christian witness. We welcome you to join us in liturgy and fellowship."

Rev. Fr. John Kizhakekkara has been serving as the spiritual father of the Odanavattom community, administering the Holy Sacraments, leading the parish council, and ensuring faith formation for the youth and catechism kids.
