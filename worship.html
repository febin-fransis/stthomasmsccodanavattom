The West Syriac Antiochene Rite

Our worship is rooted in the venerable and poetic West Syriac Liturgical tradition. It features symbolic prayer actions, deep theological chants, and profound silence. Every Holy Qurbono is structured dynamically, leading us safely into the Eucharistic celebration of communion with God.

The sacred rhythmic cycle of the year celebrating the whole mystery of Christ in the West Syriac tradition

The Liturgical Year

The Liturgical Year of the Syro-Malankara Catholic Church begins on Qudos Eetho (Sanctification of the Church) Sunday. On that day we remember the establishment and sanctification of the Church.

Throughout the liturgical cycle, the faithful journey through seven sacred seasons, entering ever more deeply into the life, death, resurrection, and teachings of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Begins: Qudos Eetho Sunday 7 Sacred Seasons

Easter & The Synod of Nicaea

The most important feast of the Liturgical Year is Easter (Qyomtho). The date of this feast changes every year according to the fullmoon that comes after the day on which the sun comes on the equator in the spring season, which is called the vernal Equinox.

In the Synod of Nicea (AD 325), it was decided to celebrate the feast of Easter on the Sunday that comes after the Passover Moon.

1. The Season of Annunciation (Suboro)

സുവോറോ കാലം (Season of Good Tidings & Expectation)
Calendar Span: Qudos Eetho Sunday (Late Oct / Early Nov) to Christmas Eve (Dec 24)

The Season of Annunciation inaugurates the Liturgical Year of the Syro-Malankara Catholic Church on Qudos Eetho (Sanctification of the Church) Sunday. It is a holy period of expectant hope, spiritual preparation, and joy as the Church meditates on God's eternal plan of salvation through the Incarnation of His Only Begotten Son.

Sanctification & Renewal: Begins with Qudos Eetho (Sanctification) followed by Hoodosh Eetho (Renewal of the Church), consecrating the parish temple and hearts of the faithful.
Angelic Announcements: Successive Sundays celebrate the Annunciation to Zechariah, the Annunciation to the Virgin Mary (Suboro), the Visitation to Elizabeth, and the Nativity of St. John the Baptist.
25-Day Christmas Fast (Yaldo Nombu): Observed with devotion from December 1 through December 25 in joyful penance and charity.
Key Feasts & Observances
Qudos Eetho Sunday 8th Sunday before Christmas • Church Sanctification
Hoodosh Eetho Sunday 7th Sunday before Christmas • Church Renewal
The Annunciation (Suboro) Commemoration of Gabriel's Message to Mary
25-Day Advent Fast December 1 – 25 (Yaldo Nombu)
"Behold, the virgin shall conceive and bear a Son, and shall call His name Immanuel." — Isaiah 7:14
Season 2 of 7 Liturgical Color: Luminous White & Festal Gold

2. The Season of Yaldo - Denho

യൽദോ - ദനഹാ കാലം (Nativity & Epiphany / Theophany)
Calendar Span: December 25 (Christmas) through Presentation of the Lord (February 2)

This luminous season celebrates the twin mysteries of the Incarnation and Divine Manifestation. First, God's descent into humble human flesh at Bethlehem (Yaldo / Christmas), and second, the manifestation of the Holy Trinity at Christ's baptism in the Jordan River (Denho / Epiphany).

The Nativity & Theeyuzhichil: Midnight Christmas Holy Qurbono accompanied by the traditional sacred bonfire (Theeyuzhichil) symbolizing Christ the Light dispelling spiritual darkness.
Epiphany (Denho / Baptism of our Lord): Celebrated on January 6 with the solemn blessing of baptismal waters. The Vicar blesses parish households with holy water.
Presentation (Mayaltho): Forty days after Christmas, Christ is carried to the temple by Mary and Joseph and met by Simeon and Anna.
Key Feasts & Observances
Yaldo (Nativity of our Lord) December 25 • Christmas Midnight Liturgy
Circumcision & Holy Name January 1 • New Year Thanksgiving
Denho (Epiphany / Theophany) January 6 • Blessing of the Waters
Mayaltho (Presentation of Jesus) February 2 • Meeting in the Temple
"The Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us. We have seen His glory!" — John 1:14
Season 3 of 7 Liturgical Color: Penitential Deep Violet & Red

3. The Season of the Great Fast

വലിയ നോമ്പ് കാലം (Saumo Rabo / 50 Days of Great Lent)
Calendar Span: Shubkono Sunday through Passion Week (Hasha) to Holy Saturday

The Great Fast is a sacred 50-day spiritual ascent of conversion, intensive prayer, fasting, and prostrations. In union with Christ's 40 days of fasting in the Judean wilderness and His Passion, the faithful purify body and spirit, culminating in the solemn commemoration of the Lord's suffering and crucifixion.

Shubkono (Service of Reconciliation): Inaugurates Lent on the first Monday with the ritual exchange of fraternal peace and forgiveness among all parishioners.
Sundays of Miracles & Mid-Lent: Meditating upon Christ's signs: the Wedding at Cana, healing the Leper, the Paralytic, the Canaanite woman, and the Man Born Blind. Mid-Lent elevates the Golgotha Cross.
Passion Week (Hasha Azhcha): Solemn liturgies from Palm Sunday (Oshana), Maundy Thursday (Pesaha with washing of feet & unleavened bread), Good Friday (Dukha Velli) burial procession, to Holy Saturday.
Key Feasts & Observances
Shubkono Sunday Entrance of Great Lent • Mutual Forgiveness
Mid-Lent (Elevation of Cross) 25th Day of Lent • Setting up Golgotha
Oshana (Palm Sunday) Blessing of Palms & Children's Procession
Pesaha & Good Friday Last Supper, Passion & Burial of our Lord
"Return to Me with all your heart, with fasting, with weeping, and with mourning." — Joel 2:12
Season 4 of 7 Liturgical Color: Radiant Resurrection White & Gold

4. The Season of Qyomtho (Easter)

ഉയിർപ്പ് കാലം (The Glorious Resurrection of our Lord)
Calendar Span: Easter Sunday to Ascension of our Lord (Suloko) (50 Paschal Days)

Qyomtho is the supreme feast and pivot of the entire Liturgical Year. Calculated according to the decree of the Synod of Nicea (AD 325) on the Sunday following the full moon after the vernal Equinox (Passover Moon), Easter proclaims Christ's definitive victory over sin, death, and hades.

Easter Night Liturgy & Peace Service: The solemn announcement of the Resurrection ("Meshiha Qom Men Qabro!"), removal of funeral veils, and the blessing with the glorious cross.
New Sunday (Puthunjayar): Celebrated on the 8th day of Easter, honoring Apostle St. Thomas touching the wounds of Christ and professing: "My Lord and My God!"
Ascension (Suloko): On the 40th day after Easter, the Church celebrates Christ ascending bodily into heaven in glory, promising to send the Holy Spirit.
Key Feasts & Observances
Easter Sunday (Qyomtho) Sunday after Passover Moon • Supreme Feast
New Sunday (Puthunjayar) St. Thomas Faith Confession Sunday
Suloko (Ascension Feast) 40th Day after Easter (Thursday)
50 Days of Paschal Joy No kneeling in liturgy during Easter season
"Meshiha Qom Men Qabro! Christ is Risen from the dead, by death trampling down death!"
Season 5 of 7 Liturgical Color: Pentecost Fire Red / Crimson

5. The Season of Pentecost

ശ്ലീഹാ കാലം / പെന്തക്കോസ്തി (Season of the Holy Spirit & Apostles)
Calendar Span: Pentecost Sunday (50th day of Easter) to the Feast of Transfiguration (August 6)

The Season of Pentecost marks the descent of God the Holy Spirit as tongues of fire upon the Apostles and Mother Mary in the Cenacle. It commemorates the birth, vitality, and universal missionary mandate of the Catholic Church. The faithful reflect on their calling to be witnesses of Christ guided by the gifts of the Spirit.

Solemn Kneeling Service: Celebrated on Pentecost Sunday with threefold profound prostrations and prayers invoking the Holy Spirit to renew the parish community.
Fast of the Apostles (Sleeha Nombu): A devotional fasting period in preparation for the feast of Saints Peter and Paul (June 29), praying for Church unity and missionaries.
Dukhrana of Apostle St. Thomas (July 3): Major parish patronal feast celebrating the martyrdom and legacy of our Father in Faith, Apostle St. Thomas in India.
Key Feasts & Observances
Pentecost Sunday 50th Day after Easter • Birthday of the Church
Fast of the Apostles Sleeha Nombu • Spiritual empowerment
Saints Peter & Paul Feast June 29 • Pillars of the Apostolic Church
Dukhrana of St. Thomas July 3 • Patronal Feast of our Parish
"They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak with other tongues as the Spirit gave them utterance." — Acts 2:4
Season 6 of 7 Liturgical Color: Luminous White & Celestial Blue

6. The Season of Transfiguration

രൂപാന്തര കാലം (M'talo / Manifestation of Divine Glory)
Calendar Span: August 6 (Transfiguration) through mid-September

The Season of Transfiguration centers upon the revelation of Christ's uncreated divine majesty on Mount Tabor before Peter, James, and John. It invites believers to behold the heavenly radiance of the Son of God, while simultaneously celebrating the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary, who was taken body and soul into heavenly glory.

Feast of the Transfiguration (Koodasha - August 6): Proclaiming Christ as the Fulfillment of the Law (Moses) and the Prophets (Elijah), prefiguring the eternal kingdom.
15-Day Assumption Fast (Shunoyo Nombu): Devoutly observed from August 1 to 15, dedicating family intentions to the maternal intercession of Mother Mary.
Sunoyo (Assumption Feast - August 15): Solemn Qurbono, veneration of Marian icons, and the traditional blessing of grain and first fruits of the soil.
Key Feasts & Observances
Transfiguration of Christ August 6 • Mount Tabor Divine Light
15-Day Assumption Lent August 1 – 15 (Shunoyo Nombu)
Sunoyo (Assumption of Mary) August 15 • Queen of Heaven
Blessing of the Harvest Offertory of seasonal agricultural fruits
"His face shone like the sun, and His clothes became as white as the light." — Matthew 17:2
Season 7 of 7 Liturgical Color: Royal Gold & Antiochene Maroon

7. The Season of Sleebo (The Holy Cross)

സ്ലീബാ കാലം (Triumph of the Cross & Second Coming)
Calendar Span: September 14 (Feast of the Cross) through Hoodosh Eetho (Late October)

The Season of Sleebo commemorates the Finding and Exaltation of the True, Life-Giving Cross of Jesus Christ. As the final season of the Liturgical Year, it draws the Church's gaze to the end of time, the Second Coming (Parousia) of Christ, the resurrection of the dead, and the eternal judgment, preparing to renew the cycle at Qudos Eetho.

Feast of the Holy Cross (Sleebo - September 14): Celebrated with a nighttime solemn cross procession bearing burning torches (Theevetti) and chanting cross hymns.
Nativity of the Virgin Mary (8-Day Lent): Celebrated on September 8 following an 8-day devotion of fasting and family prayers.
Parousia & Commemoration of All Departed: Final Sundays focus on Christian vigilance, remembering departed priests, mothers, and fathers, leading up to the new liturgical year.
Key Feasts & Observances
Nativity of Mother Mary September 8 • 8-Day Lent (Ettunombu)
Exaltation of the Holy Cross September 14 (Sleebo Feast)
Remembrance of the Dead Solemn Dhukrono prayers for departed
Closing of the Sacred Year Transition to next Qudos Eetho Sunday
